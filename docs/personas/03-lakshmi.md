# Lakshmi — Older Semi-Urban Homemaker

## Profile

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Lakshmi Palaniswamy |
| Age | 61 |
| Location | Outskirts of Madurai city (semi-urban) |
| Occupation | Homemaker; retired as a cook in a government primary school |
| Language | Tamil only — can read Tamil at a moderate pace; no English |
| Device | Older Samsung Galaxy (hand-me-down from her daughter), Android 10 |
| Connectivity | Home Wi-Fi (JioFiber installed by her son-in-law); rarely uses mobile data |
| Digital literacy | Moderate — uses WhatsApp (text and photos), watches YouTube cooking and devotional videos, struggles with web browsers and multi-tab navigation |
| Income bracket | Lower-middle (pension + family support; household income managed by children) |

## How Lakshmi consumes election information

Lakshmi's primary source is Tamil television news — she watches Thanthi TV and Sun TV debates every evening. Her family WhatsApp group shares political memes and news clips. When she wants to know something specific about a candidate, she asks her son to search Google on his phone and read the answer aloud to her. She votes diligently in every election and considers it a civic duty.

## What Lakshmi needs from the app

- Straightforward, single-level navigation — no hamburger menus, no nested dropdowns
- Constituency finder by name (she knows her area name, not her pin code or how to use a map)
- Large, readable Tamil text — she holds the phone at arm's length
- Candidate photos displayed prominently — she recognizes people by face, not by reading names quickly
- Party symbols next to candidate names — she votes by symbol at the booth

## Key accessibility considerations

- Minimum 16px Tamil body text with line-height >= 1.5 for comfortable reading
- Avoid hamburger/hidden menus — if she can't see a link, it doesn't exist for her
- Single-scroll pages preferred over paginated or tabbed layouts
- No pop-ups, modals, or "accept cookies" banners — she doesn't know how to close them and panics
- Buttons should look like buttons (visible borders, background colour) — not just underlined text
