## How Meenakshi consumes election information

Meenakshi watches Thanthi TV evening news with her family and reads Dinathanthi (Tamil newspaper) in the morning. Her school colleagues share political news in their WhatsApp group. She's particularly interested in education policy — which party will improve government school infrastructure, teacher recruitment, and student welfare. She's wary of partisan sources and values factual, neutral information.

## What Meenakshi needs from the app

- Trustworthy, non-partisan presentation of candidate information — no party bias
- Candidate education and professional background — she believes qualified candidates make better legislators
- Criminal record information displayed clearly — she discusses this with fellow teachers
- Party manifesto promises specifically related to education, if available
- Information she can reference when discussing elections with her students' parents at PTA meetings

## Key accessibility considerations

- Tamil-first interface with option to switch to English for specific data fields
- Works well on mid-range Android without lag or janky scrolling
- Content should be factual and neutral in tone — no loaded language or editorial commentary
- Pages should be bookmarkable so she can return to a specific candidate later
- Readable during her commute (auto-rickshaw) — so content should be stable, no horizontal scrolling
