## How Kavitha consumes election information

Kavitha follows Tamil political commentary channels on YouTube and scrolls through election-related Instagram infographics during her commute. She's part of several WhatsApp groups where friends share news links and candidate "scorecards." She cross-checks claims by searching Google and reading The Hindu or The News Minute articles.

## What Kavitha needs from the app

- Quick side-by-side comparison of candidates in her constituency
- Criminal cases and pending charges highlighted prominently — this is her top concern
- Shareable summary cards she can forward to her WhatsApp groups
- Asset and income declarations presented simply, not in raw legalese
- Fast toggle between Tamil and English so she can share with her parents (Tamil-only) and colleagues (English-comfortable)

## Key accessibility considerations

- Clean, modern typography that renders well on mid-range Android screens
- Fast page loads — she'll bounce if a page takes more than 3 seconds
- Responsive design that works in portrait mode during her commute
- Dark mode would reduce eye strain during late-night browsing
