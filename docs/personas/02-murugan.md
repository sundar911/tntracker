## How Murugan consumes election information

Murugan watches Tamil news channels — Sun TV, Kalaignar TV, Puthiya Thalaimurai — on the tea shop TV every evening. He receives WhatsApp voice notes and short videos from relatives and local party cadre. At the local ration shop or temple, he discusses candidates with other men from the village. He has never used a web browser or search engine.

## What Murugan needs from the app

- Entirely Tamil interface — no English labels, buttons, or untranslated text
- Very large text and high-contrast colours so he can read on his small, low-resolution screen
- Simple candidate information: photo, party, party symbol, criminal cases (yes/no), basic asset range
- Minimal data consumption — every MB counts on his Rs. 149/month Jio plan
- No forms, logins, or text input required — tap-only navigation

## Key accessibility considerations

- Touch targets minimum 48x48px — his hands are calloused from manual labour, precision tapping is hard
- Pages must work on slow 3G/edge connections when 4G drops — target under 100KB initial load
- Avoid text-heavy screens; use icons, colour coding, and candidate photos for recognition
- Content must be understandable at a 5th-grade Tamil reading level
- No pop-ups, modals, or overlay menus — they confuse users who don't know how to dismiss them
