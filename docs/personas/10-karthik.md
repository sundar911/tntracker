## How Karthik consumes election information

Karthik watches Tamil political YouTube shorts during breaks between deliveries. He's part of a Swiggy riders' union WhatsApp group where political news gets shared alongside labour rights updates. He follows a couple of political Instagram pages but mostly consumes video, not text. He cares about issues that affect his daily life: fuel prices, gig worker protections, traffic infrastructure, and healthcare.

## What Karthik needs from the app

- Extremely fast page loads — he checks his phone in 30-second breaks between deliveries, on mobile data
- Simple, app-like layout he's familiar with — card-based, vertical scroll, no complex navigation
- Candidate information relevant to working-class concerns — what have they promised about jobs, fuel, healthcare?
- Quick constituency lookup without having to type much — ideally location-based or a short dropdown
- Content that's mostly visual (photos, icons, colour-coded tags) with minimal reading required

## Key accessibility considerations

- Page weight under 100KB — he's always on mobile data and every MB matters
- Must work well on budget Android phones with 2-3GB RAM — no heavy JavaScript frameworks that cause lag
- Optimized for one-handed use in portrait mode — he often holds his phone with one hand
- Text should be concise — short labels, not paragraphs. He'll skip anything that looks like an "article"
- No interstitials, pop-ups, or cookie banners — they eat time he doesn't have
- Offline resilience — if his connection drops mid-page, what's already loaded should remain visible
