## How Priya consumes election information

Priya follows political news through Tamil Facebook groups and Instagram pages focused on social justice. She watches Puthiya Thalaimurai debates and follows Dalit and trans rights activists on X/Twitter. She attends local community meetings where political parties sometimes send representatives. She's interested in which candidates have spoken about welfare schemes, anti-discrimination policies, and healthcare access for marginalized communities.

## What Priya needs from the app

- Information on candidates' positions or track record on social welfare — especially schemes for transgender persons, Dalits, and other marginalized groups
- Inclusive, respectful language throughout the UI — no gendered assumptions in labels or greetings
- Ability to look up candidates across multiple constituencies — she works across several areas in Trichy district
- Accessible from shared computers at community centres (not just mobile)
- Clear presentation of party manifesto promises related to social welfare

## Key accessibility considerations

- Works well on both mobile and shared desktop/laptop at NGO office
- No login or personal data required — privacy matters for vulnerable community members
- Content should not assume binary gender in any labels or form fields
- Pages should be sharable via Facebook and WhatsApp (Open Graph meta tags, clean URLs)
- UI language should be neutral and welcoming — avoid exclusionary idioms
