# Thatha (Ramasamy) — Elderly Retired Farmer

## Profile

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Ramasamy (called "Thatha" — grandfather — by everyone) |
| Age | 74 |
| Location | Village near Karaikudi, Sivaganga district (rural, Chettinad region) |
| Occupation | Retired farmer; handed over the land to his sons |
| Language | Tamil only |
| Device | No personal phone — uses his grandson's Android phone when the grandson visits or hands it over |
| Connectivity | Grandson's 4G data; no Wi-Fi at home |
| Digital literacy | Minimal — can tap large buttons if shown where, but cannot type Tamil or English; needs someone to navigate to the right page for him |
| Income bracket | Lower-middle (agricultural income through sons; old-age pension Rs. 1,000/month) |

## How Thatha consumes election information

Thatha has voted in every election since 1971. His primary source is the village — conversations at the temple, the tea shop, and during evening walks. He watches Tamil news on the family TV. His grandson sometimes shows him YouTube clips of candidate speeches. He remembers candidates by face and party symbol, not by reading their names. He has strong opinions shaped by decades of local politics.

## What Thatha needs from the app

- Extremely simple interface — ideally just one tap from the home screen to see his constituency's candidates
- Large candidate photos and party symbols — these are how he identifies who's who
- Very large Tamil text (20px+ minimum) — his eyesight is declining
- No login, no account, no saved state — he uses a shared phone and shouldn't need to sign in
- Works without any text input — all navigation through tapping pre-set options

## Key accessibility considerations

- Maximum simplicity — every additional screen or step is a barrier
- Touch targets must be very large (56px+ recommended) — his fingers are thick and he taps imprecisely
- No gestures beyond basic tap — no swipe, no long-press, no pinch-to-zoom interactions
- High contrast (black text on white/light background) — avoid light greys or subtle colour differences
- The page should feel "finished" — no infinite scroll, lazy loading, or content that shifts after the page appears to load
- No English anywhere on screen — even a single English word makes him feel the app "isn't for him"
