## How Rajesh consumes election information

Rajesh reads The Hindu and Times of India apps during his morning coffee. He follows political commentators on X/Twitter and watches YouTube debates from channels like NTCC and Madan Gowri. He discusses economic policy in his Coimbatore business association WhatsApp group. He occasionally checks the ECI website directly. He's data-driven and wants numbers, not narratives.

## What Rajesh needs from the app

- Detailed candidate financial data — assets, liabilities, income sources, and growth over time
- Constituency-level historical voting trends and margins
- Quick, reliable Tamil/English toggle — he switches depending on context
- Data-dense views that don't waste screen space with oversized cards or excessive whitespace
- Fast API responses — he's impatient with slow-loading sites

## Key accessibility considerations

- Responsive design that works well on both his iPhone and office laptop/wide screen
- Tables and charts should be legible and well-formatted on all screen sizes
- Support for iOS Safari quirks (the app should be tested on WebKit)
- Quick-access search or constituency dropdown — he doesn't want to scroll through lists
- Print-friendly or exportable candidate summaries he can share in his business association group
